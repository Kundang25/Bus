# PRODUCT VISION — STRIDE

**Tagline:** Every stop. Every passenger. One effortless flow.

## Concept Overview
STRIDE is a mobile-first digital ticketing companion engineered specifically for bus conductors operating in fast-paced, high-vibration, and daylight-glare urban transit environments (such as BMTC in Bengaluru and BEST in Mumbai).

## Core Philosophy
**Speed without confusion. Simplicity without sacrificing control.**

## Key Objectives
1. **Under-4-Second Ticketing:** Enable conductors to select a destination and issue a 3-passenger ticket in under 4 seconds.
2. **One-Handed Thumb Ergonomics:** Place 85% of primary actions inside the natural thumb arc for effortless single-handed operation while holding cash in the other hand.
3. **High Sunlight Contrast:** Deliver readable typography and high contrast for outdoor daylight conditions.
4. **Offline Resilience:** Guarantee uninterrupted operation with IndexedDB client persistence when network signals drop inside transit tunnels or dense corridors.
