# PROBLEM ANALYSIS — Nielsen Bus Ticketing Challenge

## The Problem Statement
Traditional handheld thermal printer ticketing machines suffer from:
- Heavy physical weight (~700g) causing hand fatigue
- Worn physical keypads requiring heavy pressure
- Low-resolution reflective screens unreadable in sunlight
- Multi-step menu trees slowing down ticket issuance per passenger
- Vulnerability to paper jams and network disconnects

## Physical Operating Context
Bus conductors work in demanding physical environments:
- **Crowded & Moving Vehicle:** Continuous vibration, sudden braking, standing passengers pushing against the conductor.
- **Single Free Hand:** One hand holds cash notes/coins; the other operates the device.
- **Split-Second Decisions:** Serving 50+ passengers within short stop intervals.
- **Lighting Extremes:** Dark interiors shifting to blinding outdoor glare through bus windows.
