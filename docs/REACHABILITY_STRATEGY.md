# REACHABILITY & THUMB ERGONOMICS STRATEGY

## Zone Classification
STRIDE divides the mobile viewport (390px × 844px) into three ergonomic zones based on single-thumb arc sweeps:

### 1. Natural Thumb Zone (Green Arc — Bottom 40%)
- **Location:** Lower viewport reachable without flexing or stretching the thumb.
- **Controls Placed Here:**
  - Massive `ISSUE TICKET (3 PAX - ₹30)` Action Button
  - Fast Passenger Presets (1, 2, 3, 4, 5)
  - Payment Mode Selector (Cash, UPI, Pass)
  - Fast Cash Change Calculator Trigger

### 2. Stretch Zone (Yellow Arc — Middle 35%)
- **Location:** Middle viewport requiring slight thumb extension.
- **Controls Placed Here:**
  - Frequent Destination Stop Quick Grid
  - Destination Search Bar & Scrollable Stop List

### 3. Hard Reach Zone (Red Arc — Top 25%)
- **Location:** Top viewport requiring hand re-grip or two-handed reach.
- **Controls Placed Here:**
  - Static Route Number & Direction Status
  - Network Signal & Battery Indicators

## Handedness Support
STRIDE provides instant toggling between **Right-Hand Layout** and **Left-Hand Layout**, adjusting the primary action button arc for natural left-thumb reach.
