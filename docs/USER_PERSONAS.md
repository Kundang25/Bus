# USER PERSONAS — Bus Conductor Operations

## Persona 1: Ramesh Kumar (BMTC Route 500-D Conductor)
- **Age:** 42
- **Experience:** 14 years on high-density Outer Ring Road buses in Bengaluru.
- **Physical Working Conditions:** Stands inside packed buses serving 60+ standing commuters per stop. Holds cash notes between fingers of his left hand.
- **Primary Need:** Needs to issue 3-passenger tickets in under 4 seconds with his right thumb without looking at a complex menu tree.
- **Pain Points:** Traditional thermal machines jam, keys get worn out, and small text is unreadable in outdoor glare.

## Persona 2: Sunita Patil (BEST Route 332 Conductor)
- **Age:** 36
- **Experience:** 9 years in Mumbai urban routes.
- **Primary Need:** Prefers left-handed operation and clear payment mode differentiation (Cash vs UPI QR vs Pass).
- **Pain Points:** Sudden bus braking causes mis-taps; needs large touch targets (min 48px/56px).
